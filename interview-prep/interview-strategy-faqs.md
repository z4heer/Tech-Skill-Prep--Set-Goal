# Strategic Interview FAQ: Health Break, Freelancing & Salary Negotiation

---

## 1. Good Reason for Taking a Break from Full-Time Job

**Health & Wellness Focus:**

- "I decided to take a break from my full-time role to prioritize my health and well-being. This period allowed me to recover, recharge, and refocus my energy."
- "During my break, I adopted a healthier lifestyle, focused on mental wellness, and invested time in personal growth, which has made me more productive and resilient."
- "This pause helped me reassess my career goals and return to work with renewed motivation and clarity."

**Transitioning to Freelance:**

- "Freelancing offers flexibility to manage my schedule around health needs, enabling me to deliver high-quality work without compromising my wellness."
- "It provides an opportunity to choose projects aligning with my expertise and personal circumstances, ensuring better work-life balance."
- "Freelance work allows me to stay updated with new technologies and engage with diverse clients, which has enriched my skills and professional network."

---

## 2. Salary Expectation to Ask (INR)

- For an experienced Full Stack Developer (Java/Python, 18+ years, Pune) transitioning to freelance:
  - **Hourly Rate (INR):** ₹2,000 – ₹3,500 per hour (based on project complexity and skill)
  - **Monthly Retainer (INR):** ₹3,00,000 – ₹5,00,000 per month (full-time equivalent contract)
  - **Per Project (INR):** ₹1,50,000 – ₹7,00,000+ per project (depending on scope, duration, client size)
- **Best Practice:** Research market rates on Upwork, Freelancer, Naukri, LinkedIn; adjust for your experience and the client’s size/location.

**How to Communicate:**
> “Based on my experience and current industry standards, my expectation is in the range of ₹2,50,000 to ₹4,00,000 per month for full-time freelance engagement, or ₹2,500 per hour for project-based work. I’m open to discussing structure and scope for a mutually beneficial arrangement.”

---

## 3. Questions to Ask During Interview

### About Work & Projects
- What are the key responsibilities and deliverables for this role?
- What technologies and frameworks are actively used in current projects?
- What are the biggest technical challenges the team is facing?
- How are projects prioritized and assigned to freelancers?
- Is there flexibility in choosing projects or working hours?

### About the Company & Culture
- Can you describe the company’s culture and team collaboration style?
- How does the company support remote workers/freelancers?
- Are there opportunities for professional development or training?
- What is the company’s vision for growth and innovation?

### About Terms & Engagement
- What is the typical contract duration and renewal process?
- How is work performance evaluated for freelancers?
- What are the payment terms (frequency, method, invoicing)?
- Is there a notice period or exit clause in freelance contracts?
- Are there expectations regarding availability, meetings, or response times?
- Will I have access to company resources, tools, or cloud accounts?

### About Support & Onboarding
- What onboarding process is followed for new freelancers?
- Who will be the primary point of contact for technical and administrative support?
- Is there a feedback mechanism or regular review meetings?

---

## 4. Tips for Discussion

- Be open about your health journey and how freelancing supports your productivity.
- Clearly state your salary expectations, but show flexibility for long-term or high-value clients.
- Ask questions to ensure the work environment aligns with your values and lifestyle.
- Evaluate company stability, payment reliability, and scope for future growth.

---

**Summary:**  
Taking a break for health reasons is a responsible choice, and freelancing offers flexible, high-value opportunities. Approaching salary negotiation confidently and asking the right questions will help you secure the best terms for your next engagement.
